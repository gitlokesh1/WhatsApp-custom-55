Automated Engagement

The backend automatically sends high-converting milestones to active users:

  • 10 Messages Sent: Congratulatory milestone boost to maintain daily streak.
  • Every 25 Messages: Encouraging alert displaying current balance and withdrawal proximity.
  • Balance Milestone: Notifies users when they reach threshold balance to complete final tasks.
Firebase Setup

Provide FIREBASE_PROJECT_ID and FIREBASE_SERVICE_ACCOUNT_JSON in environment variables. If not set, notifications are safely recorded in simulation mode.
