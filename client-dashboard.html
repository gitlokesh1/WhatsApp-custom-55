Campaign Lifecycle & Guide

How 88Task routes your campaigns securely:

  1. Consented Audience: CSV must contain phone, message, and consent (1, yes, or true).
  2. Admin Review & Pricing: Rates are quoted per target country currency.
  3. User Distribution: Tasks are automatically matched to verified real Android app devices in the target region.
  4. Single-Device Execution: Each task is securely claimed and sent by one user.
  5. Proof & Reporting: Live progress updates below with downloadable delivery audit reports.
