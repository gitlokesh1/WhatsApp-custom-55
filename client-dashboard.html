How approval works

Every campaign follows the same controlled process.

  1. You upload consented recipients and message content.
  2. Our team validates the data and assigns the channel price.
  3. Approved tasks are activated for eligible users in the selected country.
  4. Each task can be claimed and completed by only one user.
  5. Progress updates below as tasks complete.
