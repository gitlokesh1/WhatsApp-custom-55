Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 September 2026

This policy explains how 88Task collects, uses, shares, and protects information when you create an account, connect WhatsApp, complete tasks, earn rewards, request withdrawals, use referrals, or contact support.

Information we collect

We collect your name, generated User ID, country, referral relationship, account and reward activity, support messages, and security records. When you connect WhatsApp, we process the phone number and technical session identifiers needed to provide the connection. Withdrawal requests may include beneficiary and payment details required by the selected payout channel. We also receive basic device, browser, IP address, timestamp, and diagnostic information. Your language choice is stored in your browser.

How we use information

We use information to operate and secure accounts, match country-eligible tasks, verify task completion, calculate rewards and referral commissions, process withdrawals, prevent fraud and abuse, provide support, meet legal obligations, and improve reliability. We do not sell personal information.

Sharing and service providers

Information may be shared only as needed with infrastructure, database, security, communications, analytics, and payment providers; country-specific customer-service teams can access tickets only for their assigned country. We may disclose information when required by law, to protect users or the service, or during a business transfer subject to appropriate safeguards. WhatsApp is a third-party service governed by its own terms and privacy practices.

Retention and security

We keep account, ledger, consent, fraud-prevention, and transaction records for as long as needed to provide the service, resolve disputes, and satisfy legal or accounting duties. Other information is deleted or anonymized when no longer needed. We use access controls, restricted support links, hashed credentials and tokens, and encrypted transport, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices and rights

You may choose your language, disconnect WhatsApp, and ask support to access, correct, export, restrict, or delete eligible personal information. Some financial, security, or legal records must be retained. The service is not intended for children under 18. Information may be processed in countries other than yours with reasonable safeguards.

Contact and changes

Submit a ticket through the Support page for privacy questions or requests. We may update this policy as the service changes; the revised date will be shown here, and material changes may be announced in the application. Continued use after an update means the revised policy applies, where permitted by law.
