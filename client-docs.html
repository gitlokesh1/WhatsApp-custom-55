Campaign documentation

Everything needed to prepare, submit, and monitor SMS and WhatsApp task campaigns.

1. Access and account security

  • Our admin team creates your login ID and provides the initial password privately.
  • Do not share credentials or recipient files through public links.
  • Contact operations if access should be disabled or the password reset.

2. Required CSV format

Use UTF-8 CSV with one recipient per row. The title column is optional.

phone,message,consent,title
919876543210,"Your approved message",yes,Launch audience 1
919111111111,"Your approved message",true,Launch audience 2
  • phone: international digits, 7–15 digits; a leading + is accepted.
  • message: required, maximum 4,096 characters.
  • consent: must be yes, true, or 1.
  • Duplicate phone-and-message rows within one upload are skipped.
  • Maximum 50,000 data rows per campaign.

3. Review and pricing

  1. Submission enters Pending review and cannot be claimed by users.
  2. Operations checks country, channel, consent, content, and recipient formatting.
  3. Only operations can assign SMS or WhatsApp pricing. Clients cannot submit or change prices.
  4. Once status becomes Active, eligible users can claim tasks.

4. Delivery and completion

  • Each CSV row becomes one task and can be reserved by only one user at a time.
  • A successfully sent task is permanently removed from all users’ available lists.
  • Failed or expired attempts can be made available again.
  • The campaign dashboard shows total, reserved, completed, and failed attempts.
  • SMS “sent” means the Android device submitted all message parts to the carrier; it does not guarantee handset delivery.

5. Content responsibilities

  • Upload only recipients with valid consent for the selected channel and purpose.
  • Do not upload unlawful, deceptive, abusive, or prohibited content.
  • Do not include passwords, payment-card data, government IDs, or unnecessary sensitive information.
  • Operations may pause or reject campaigns that fail review.
